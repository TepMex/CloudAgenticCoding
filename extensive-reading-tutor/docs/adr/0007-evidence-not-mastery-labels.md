# Memory is evidence, not a label the model may confirm

A lesson records what the learner showed on that attempt and what help was actually on screen. The knowledge row is a projection of those units. Prepare may see a short, language-filtered support summary. Evaluate does not receive mastery labels or earlier errors, so the grade is not a search for a prior weakness.

An independent success is one evidence unit: the same attempt, concept, and skill collapse to a single count even when the model emits several quotes. Help that disclosed a meaning, or whose scope cannot be tied to one question, blocks that unit. Three successes on one day or in one repeated context do not become `STABLE`. The v2 thresholds — distinct contexts, study days, and a seven-day span — are versioned heuristics in `policy-v2`, not a measured forgetting curve. A self-report of difficulty plans support and does not by itself remove `STABLE`.

The same lesson cannot be read as a language level. Reading comprehension, recognition of an expression, contextual inference, and active production stay separate, and a Russian retelling of Chinese does not update active production. A declared HSK band is a preference, not a result. v1 lessons keep the v1 evaluator; new lessons use strategy-v2.
