ctx-calendar

Month calendar with your photos on each day.

Android app (API 34+): Google Calendar–style month view where each day shows gallery thumbnails from pictures taken that day. Tap a day for a photo grid, tap a photo for full screen.

Download ctx-calendar.apk

Updating: install the new APK over the existing app. If Android blocks the install, uninstall once and reinstall—only needed when the signing key changed from an older build.
