Paint the map by walking — GPS brush on OpenStreetMap

Android app (API 34+): open an OpenStreetMap, see your GPS position, and press Start to paint the world around you. Moving draws brush strokes; staying deepens color intensity. Drawings are stored efficiently on-device and can be exported from Settings.
