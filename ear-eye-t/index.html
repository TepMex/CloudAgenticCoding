ear-eye-t

Unlocked screen time and playing sound, kept apart.

Android app for a personal phone: confirmed time the screen was unlocked, confirmed time a chosen kind of audio was playing, their overlap, and the gaps where observation stopped. Data stays on the device. Russian UI.

Download ear-eye-t.apk

Updating: install the new APK over the existing app. If Android blocks the install, uninstall once and reinstall—only needed when the signing key changed from an older build. Uninstalling deletes the local history.
